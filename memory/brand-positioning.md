OnlineHotelier exists to be **the person a hotelier can ask**. It helps Indian hotels explore and understand the digital landscape: listing on OTAs, how those channels work, how direct booking works, choosing software, and planning next steps. It is a consultant and guide, not a services vendor.

**Why:** Confirmed by the user on 2026-07-27. A first draft of the main-domain homepage was written agency-style (hero "More Bookings. Lower Commissions." → six service cards → hire us). The user rejected the framing: that page could belong to any OTA listing agency in India and buried the real differentiator. The 57 free guides and 11 free calculators ARE the proof of the consultant positioning — giving away substantial expertise is evidence of being a guide rather than a vendor.

**How to apply:**
- Lead with the hotelier's situation and confusion, never with what you sell.
- Services appear as the "or let us do it for you" option woven into each topic area, roughly 60%+ down a page, never as the headline.
- Free guides and tools are the offer, not supporting proof for a sales pitch.
- Keep the conversion path short anyway: WhatsApp in hero, service links inside each area, closing CTA. Softening the sell must not mean hiding it.
- Honesty as positioning: say when the answer is "nothing yet" or "you don't need this". See the "Not Sure What You Need?" card.
- Voice: helping, exploring, planning — the verbs from the user's own description.

Reference implementation: `src/public/home-main.html` (hero → self-select router → five areas → tools → done-for-you → why we exist → FAQ → CTA).
