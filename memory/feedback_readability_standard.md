All guides must pass this checklist before publishing. Target audience: Indian independent hotel owner, 15–50 rooms, new to OTAs and hotel software.

**Why:** The site targets beginners and early-stage hoteliers in India, not revenue managers or chain-hotel executives. Jargon and assumed knowledge cause high bounce rates and zero conversions from search.

**How to apply:** Run through this checklist on every guide before commit. Applies to all content, especially OTA, software, and compliance guides.

---

## Readability Checklist

1. **No unexplained jargon.** Every technical term explained in parentheses on first use:
   - "extranet (the OTA's partner dashboard where you manage your listing)"
   - "rate parity (keeping the same price across all booking channels)"
   - "AP/CP/MAP (meal plan types: American Plan, Continental Plan, Modified American Plan)"
   - "channel manager (software that syncs your availability across all OTAs at once)"

2. **Rates and numbers in ₹.** Never use percentages alone. Always show a ₹ example:
   - "18% commission" → "18% commission — on a ₹3,000 booking, that is ₹540"
   - Use a consistent base (e.g., ₹3,000, ₹5,000) across a whole guide for easy comparison

3. **Story-first opening.** Every guide opens with a scene or relatable situation — a hotelier in a tier-2 city facing the problem — not a definition or a heading.

4. **Short sentences.** No sentence longer than 25 words. Use full stops, not commas, to break long thoughts.

5. **"You" framing.** Address the reader directly. "Your hotel", "your guests", "you will save" — not "hotels should" or "properties can".

6. **Reading level target: 8th grade or below.** Simple words over technical words. "Stop" not "cease". "Start" not "initiate". "Use" not "utilise".

7. **Bold `<strong>channel manager</strong>` every instance** — it is the most important tool concept on the site and deserves emphasis.

8. **"New to X? Start here" callout** at the top of every advanced guide. Link to the foundational guide for that topic.

9. **No em dashes (—).** Use commas, full stops, colons, or parentheses instead. [[feedback_writing_style]]
