What is TDS on OTA Payments?

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) under Section 194-O requires e-commerce operators (OTAs) to deduct tax when making payments to hotel sellers.

Aspect Details
Section 194-O of Income Tax Act
Rate 0.1% (5% if no valid PAN is on file)
Deducted by OTA (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, etc.)
Deducted on Gross room value before commission, excluding GST shown separately on the invoice
When At the time of credit or payment, whichever is earlier

What is TCS on OTA Payments?

TCS (Tax Collected at Source) on OTA bookings is a GST provision. Under Section 52 of the CGST Act, an e-commerce operator such as MakeMyTrip or Booking.com collects a small share of each booking's value from you and deposits it against your GSTIN. It is not the income tax TCS under Section 206C(1H): that section covered sales of goods, never hotel rooms, and it stopped applying on 1 April 2025. For the current GST rates on hotel rooms (5% and 18%) see the GST on hotel rooms guide.

Aspect Details
Section Section 52 of the CGST Act, 2017
Rate 0.5% (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST, or 0.5% IGST). Cut from 1% on 10 July 2024
Collected by OTA, against your GSTIN
Collected on Net taxable value of the room (before GST)
Threshold None. It applies from the first booking
Where it shows GST portal, from the OTA's monthly GSTR-8 return
Not GST-registered? Then there is no TCS. For a hotel that is not registered for GST, the OTA pays GST on the room itself under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act, as the deemed supplier. Check your contract and settlement statement to see which treatment your OTA applies.

TDS vs TCS: Key Differences

Factor TDS (Section 194-O) TCS (GST Section 52)
Which tax Income tax GST
Rate 0.1% 0.5%
Linked to Your PAN Your GSTIN
Threshold None for companies and firms None
Credit used against Income tax, in your ITR GST liability, via the electronic cash ledger
Reflects in Form 26AS / AIS GST portal (TCS credit from GSTR-8)

How to Claim TDS/TCS Credit

  1. TDS: check Form 26AS. The OTA's 0.1% TDS appears against your PAN on the Income Tax portal after it files its quarterly return
  2. TDS: claim in your ITR. Reconcile 26AS against the OTA's TDS certificates (Form 16A), then claim the credit when filing
  3. TCS: accept it on the GST portal. The OTA's 0.5% TCS appears from its monthly GSTR-8 return. Accept it and it moves to your electronic cash ledger
  4. TCS: use it. Pay your GST from the cash ledger, or claim a refund of any excess balance
Pro Tip: Download TDS certificates and monthly TCS statements from each OTA extranet. Keep them for reconciliation and audit purposes.

What If PAN is Not Provided?

Scenario TDS Rate (194-O)
PAN provided and valid 0.1%
PAN not provided or invalid 5%
50x higher deduction. Without a valid PAN on file, the OTA deducts 5% instead of 0.1%: ₹250 instead of ₹5 on a ₹5,000 booking. Check that your PAN is registered correctly with every OTA. GST TCS works off your GSTIN, so it stays at 0.5% either way.

Common Issues & Solutions

1. TDS not reflecting in Form 26AS, or TCS missing on the GST portal

  • TDS appears quarterly, after the OTA files its TDS return. TCS appears monthly, after it files GSTR-8
  • Contact OTA partner support with booking references
  • Check that the PAN and GSTIN in the OTA extranet match your registrations

2. Mismatch between OTA statement and Form 26AS

  • Verify OTA's TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number)
  • Check for timing differences (accrual vs payment basis)
  • Raise ticket with OTA's finance team

3. Double deduction concerns

  • TDS and TCS are different provisions under two different taxes, so both apply
  • TDS is credited against income tax; TCS against GST
  • You're not losing money - it's advance tax payment
Remember: TDS and TCS are not additional costs - they're advance payments. You claim TDS back in your income tax return and use TCS to pay your GST. If you never claim them, though, they do become a cost, so reconcile every month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TDS for hotels in India?
TDS stands for Tax Deducted at Source. Hotels see it in two places. OTAs deduct 0.1% under Section 194-O on every booking they pay out. Companies that book rooms on a regular basis, under a rate contract, deduct 10% under Section 194-I once their payments to your hotel cross ₹50,000 in a month. One-off bookings by a company and individual leisure travellers do not attract TDS. Give your PAN to every OTA and corporate client so the credit shows up in your Form 26AS.
What is TCS on OTA bookings for hotels?
TCS stands for Tax Collected at Source. On OTA bookings it is a GST provision, Section 52 of the CGST Act, not an income tax one. OTAs like MakeMyTrip, Booking.com and Agoda collect 0.5% of the net room value (before GST) from GST-registered hotels and deposit it against the hotel's GSTIN. The rate was cut from 1% to 0.5% on 10 July 2024. The credit lands in your GST electronic cash ledger, not Form 26AS.
What is the difference between TDS and TCS for hotels?
On an OTA booking, TDS is income tax: the OTA deducts 0.1% under Section 194-O against your PAN, and you claim it in your income tax return. TCS is GST: the OTA collects 0.5% under Section 52 of the CGST Act against your GSTIN, and you use it to pay your GST liability. Both come out of the same payout, but they are claimed back in two different places.
How do hotels claim TCS credit from OTAs?
OTAs file a monthly GSTR-8 return showing the 0.5% TCS they collected against your GSTIN. You can see it on the GST portal, and once you accept it the amount is credited to your electronic cash ledger. You can then use it to pay your GST or claim it as a refund. Check that the GSTIN in each OTA extranet matches your registration, or the credit will not reach you.
