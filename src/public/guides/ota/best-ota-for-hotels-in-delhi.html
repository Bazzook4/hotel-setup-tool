How We Score OTA Channels

Presence leads because corporate travellers and transit guests search by location first. A channel that hides you in a Gurugram or Aerocity search earns nothing. Cleartrip is ranked fourth nationally (STAAH 2025) and reaches domestic tech-savvy travellers in Gurugram via its Flipkart app flight-to-hotel cross-sell; it scores 3.0 across all three criteria. Delhi NCR's Demand Profile: Transit, Embassy, Corporate and Exhibition

Aerocity layover and crew demand. IGI Airport handles approximately 10.6 crore passengers annually, with T3 handling over 2.1 crore international passengers in 2024 (Swarajya Mag, 2025). A dedicated hospitality zone adjacent to T3 includes branded properties from JW Marriott, Pullman, Novotel and ibis. Transit guests and crew drive year-round demand here.

Embassy and diplomatic demand. Chanakyapuri holds over 60 embassies and High Commissions. Diplomatic staff and UN personnel generate steady demand in CP, Lutyens Delhi and Aerocity. At volume this is negotiated-rate territory, not OTA territory.

Gurugram corporate demand. Cyber City, Golf Course Road and Udyog Vihar house IT, fintech and MNC regional headquarters. Flexible workspace leasing in NCR rose 65% in Q2 2025, with large enterprises at 55 to 60% of demand (CorporatEdge, realtynmore.com, 2025).

Exhibition demand. Yashobhoomi (IICC, Dwarka Sector 25) holds up to 11,000 delegates (Wikipedia, 2026). Bharat Mandapam hosts 70-plus exhibitions annually (Wikipedia, 2024). India Expo Mart (IEML), Greater Noida, hosts India ITME 2026 (4 to 9 December 2026, fibre2fashion.com). These three venues create sharp demand spikes. For the international source-market breakdown, see the guide to attracting international guests.

MakeMyTrip and Goibibo for Delhi Hotels

One MMT listing distributes across Goibibo and Travelguru automatically. For domestic corporate travel, weekend leisure and pilgrimage transit guests, MMT is the primary search tool. Commission runs 15 to 25%; on a ₹5,500 room that is ₹825 to ₹1,375 per booking. SME travel managers in Gurugram use myBiz (MakeMyTrip's corporate booking tool) to book from a curated list; set up your amenities and GST invoicing in Connect by MakeMyTrip and that traffic finds you automatically. During exhibition weeks, activate MMTBLACK member rates with breakfast to hold rate while pushing volume.

Booking.com for Delhi Hotels

Booking.com is the primary channel for European independent travellers on the Golden Triangle circuit (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur). For Aerocity and Connaught Place business hotels, Genius tier eligibility is the key lever: Genius members from Europe and North America book in advance and carry high average rates.

Booking for Business handles MNC travel desks that need GST-compliant invoicing. Activate short-stay rates (under 12 hours) on your Aerocity listing to appear in layover and transit searches. Use the Visibility Booster during inter-event gap periods when the next exhibition surge is weeks away.

Expedia and Hotels.com for Delhi Hotels

Expedia's strength in Delhi is transit and diplomatic travel. North American and European guests on layover at IGI Terminal 3 book through Expedia Group supply, directly on Expedia.com or via managed travel programmes. Embassy teams and international government delegations follow this route. Delhi's Expedia frame is airport proximity and diplomatic context, not BFSI deal-closing (Mumbai) or GCC tech managed travel (Hyderabad).

Hotels.com earns its place as a repeat-stay loyalty tool for diplomatic and expat staff who rotate through Delhi regularly. Listing on both surfaces your property to the same Expedia Group buyers without a second extranet. Use TravelAds and the Accelerator for gap-fill between exhibition events.

Agoda for Delhi Hotels

Agoda's primary strength in Delhi is Southeast Asian source markets: Singapore, Malaysia, South Korea and Japan. South Korean and Japanese travellers on the Golden Triangle circuit transit through Delhi before Agra and Jaipur. For hotels in Karol Bagh and Paharganj serving budget-to-mid-scale international guests, Agoda is the second international channel after Booking.com.

Gulf travellers from UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait use both Agoda and Booking.com for Delhi; do not assume Agoda leads. List on both and let your booking-source data decide after 60 days. Manage Agoda through the YCS (Your Connectivity Solutions) extranet. For South Delhi boutique properties (Hauz Khas, Lodi Colony), check the current B&B position with Delhi Tourism before listing on Airbnb: the 2007 Act's repeal awaits notification and a new policy is in draft.

Direct Booking in Delhi NCR: GeM Registration, Google Free Links and Corporate Rate Sheets

Delhi has three direct-booking channels uniquely scalable here.

  • GeM registration: DGS&D was wound up on 31 October 2017 (procurementinet.org). All central government and PSU procurement now runs through GeM (Government e-Marketplace) under General Financial Rules, 2017. Register in the Hotels and Accommodation category. PSUs and central ministries float rate-contract tenders on GeM; submit a bid for a two-year period. Zero commission once signed.
  • Embassy rate agreements: Approach travel desks in Chanakyapuri directly with a rate sheet. One agreement can mean 20 to 40 room nights per month, with GST invoicing as the main requirement.
  • Google Free Booking Links and corporate rate sheets: Connect your booking engine to Google Hotel Free Booking Links (zero commission). For Gurugram, two or three Cyber City corporate agreements remove commission on your most consistent revenue. Offer ₹5,800 direct against your ₹7,500 OTA rate for confirmed blocks.

A channel manager keeps rates and availability in sync across all platforms when you are running OTAs alongside GeM contracts and corporate blocks. Read the channel manager guide before going live on more than two platforms. For GST rate guidance on your tariff, check the GST on hotel rooms guide.

Which Channels to Prioritise by Delhi NCR Area

Aerocity and IGI Terminal 3 precinct

This precinct runs on transit logic: short stays, last-minute bookings and crew rotations. Booking.com and Expedia lead for international transit guests; activate short-stay rates on both. Agoda serves ME and SE Asian layover guests. Keep cancellation policy tight for same-day bookings. Expedia punches above its city-wide score here because of the North American and European transit volume through T3.

Gurugram: Cyber City and Golf Course Road

Booking for Business on Booking.com and MMT for domestic corporate are the two workhorses. myBiz routes SME travel managers from smaller Gurugram companies to MMT-listed hotels; confirm your amenity list and GST invoicing are accurate. Direct corporate rate sheets close the highest-volume accounts: three Cyber City agreements can fill 30 to 40 room nights per week at zero commission.

Dwarka/Yashobhoomi and Noida/India Expo Mart

Both zones follow an exhibition-spike pattern. Yashobhoomi hosts major conventions including Fire India 2026 (28 to 30 September 2026, globaltradefairs.com). IEML hosts India ITME 2026 (4 to 9 December 2026, fibre2fashion.com). Check event calendars 60 days ahead, raise rates 25 to 40% during large shows, and activate Booking.com Genius and MMT SmartDeals for inter-event gaps. Noida International Airport (Jewar) opened domestic operations in June 2026 (outlooktraveller.com), giving hotels on the Yamuna Expressway a second airport proximity argument. MMT captures more exhibition delegate traffic here than city average because Indian B2B delegates search MMT first.

Rewari note. Rewari, on the NCR's south-western fringe, serves the Bawal and Dharuhera industrial belt: automotive manufacturing including Hero MotoCorp's plant (autocarpro.in, 2013). Rewari is Haryana's second-largest GST contributor at ₹3,271 crore (newswav.com, 2026). MMT and Goibibo dominate here; Booking.com's international reach adds little here. Offer Bawal plant admin teams a monthly rate sheet. A clean MMT listing with corporate-friendly amenities and GST-invoicing capability is the starting point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which OTA gives a Delhi hotel the best visibility for Aerocity transit guests?
Booking.com and Expedia lead for Aerocity transit guests. Booking.com reaches European travellers routing through IGI Terminal 3 on the Golden Triangle circuit. Expedia and Hotels.com serve North American transit guests and diplomatic travellers who book through managed travel programmes. Activate short-stay rates (under 12 hours) on both platforms to appear in layover searches.
Do I need Delhi Tourism Department registration before listing my hotel on MakeMyTrip or Booking.com?
Standard hotels in Delhi do not need a separate Delhi Tourism Department registration before listing. The Delhi B&B Act 2007 covered only residential bed-and-breakfasts, and the Assembly passed its repeal in August 2026. Before listing, confirm you have: GST registration, FSSAI licence (if F&B), Fire NOC and an MCD or NDMC trade licence. Delhi Police licensing for hotels ended in June 2025. Ministry of Tourism star classification is voluntary.
How do I get government and PSU bookings for my Delhi hotel without going through an OTA?
Register on GeM (Government e-Marketplace) as a supplier in the Hotels and Accommodation category. Central ministries and PSUs float hotel rate-contract tenders on GeM; submit a rate bid for a fixed period. GST registration and FSSAI licence are standard tender requirements. For embassy bookings, approach travel desks in Chanakyapuri directly with a rate agreement.
Is Booking.com or Agoda better for Gulf travellers visiting Delhi?
Both channels serve Gulf travellers to Delhi. Booking.com likely has the larger share for UAE and Saudi visitors. Agoda reaches Gulf demand alongside its stronger Southeast Asian markets: Singapore, Malaysia, South Korea and Japan. List on both and review your booking-source report after 60 days to see which drives more Gulf traffic for your property.
My hotel is near Yashobhoomi or India Expo Mart. How do I handle the demand spike during exhibitions?
Check the event calendar for Yashobhoomi (Dwarka Sector 25) and India Expo Mart (Greater Noida) at least 60 days ahead. Raise rates and tighten cancellation policies in the 10 days around large shows. Activate MMT SmartDeals and Booking.com Genius for inter-event gaps. A channel manager keeps inventory accurate across platforms when you make rapid rate changes.
How should a Gurugram hotel use MakeMyTrip for corporate bookings?
Set up your corporate profile in Connect by MakeMyTrip with accurate amenities: Wi-Fi, GST invoicing, express check-out. myBiz (MakeMyTrip's corporate booking tool) routes SME travel managers to listed hotels automatically. For large MNC desks in Cyber City, also set up Booking for Business on Booking.com, which handles GST-compliant invoicing for international corporate accounts.
