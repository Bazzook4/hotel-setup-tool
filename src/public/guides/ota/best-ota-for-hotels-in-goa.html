By OnlineHotelier Insights Team Updated October 2026 8 min read

Goa OTA Rankings at a Glance

5 channels + direct, weighted for Goa's guest mix

Goa recorded 1.08 crore tourist arrivals in 2025: roughly 1.03 crore domestic and 5.18 lakh foreign (Goa Tourism, Jan 2026). The foreign 5% stays longer and pays more per night. Your hotel in Anjuna and a beach resort in Palolem need different OTA strategies. The guest in Anjuna in November is often Israeli or European, found you on Booking.com, and is planning 2 weeks. The family in Palolem in December came from Pune via MakeMyTrip. Same state, completely different channels.

How We Score OTA Channels

Three criteria, scored 1-5, with Goa-specific weights. International guests are a small share of Goa's arrivals but a disproportionate share of revenue for most properties, so Booking.com presence scores more heavily here than a national ranking would suggest.

  • Promotional tools (30%): deal types, loyalty rates, visibility programmes and ads available in the hotel's dashboard.
  • Presence in Goa (40%): how many of Goa's actual guest source markets that channel reaches. Domestic India books heavily on MMT/Goibibo; UK/Europe/Israel books on Booking.com; villas and long-stay guests lean to Airbnb.
  • Presentation and categories (30%): property types, badges, and filters a Goa property can qualify for. Weighted higher here because Goa's leisure guests browse by category (Beachfront, Couple-Friendly, Villas) more than city guests do.

Goa's Guest Source-Market Mix

Domestic travellers dominate by volume. Most come from Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi-NCR, booking on MMT and Goibibo. Peak domestic periods: Christmas and New Year, Diwali, and long weekends. International guests represent a small share of total arrivals, but average longer stays and higher daily spend.

Russian guests do not book on OTAs. Booking.com, Expedia, and Airbnb suspended Russia operations in 2022. Russian demand for Goa comes via charter tour operators: Aeroflot ran weekly services from Moscow, Novosibirsk, and Yekaterinburg into Manohar International Airport (Mopa) for the 2025-26 season, opening 2 October 2025. To reach this market, build a direct relationship with Goa-based inbound operators who hold charter contracts.

MakeMyTrip and Goibibo for Goa Hotels

Listing on one automatically lists on both: they share a single inventory platform. For the domestic segment, this is Goa's highest-volume channel. Calangute, Baga, and Candolim fill largely through MMT. Domestic demand around Dec-Jan peak, Diwali, and festival weekends runs almost entirely through MMT and Goibibo.

Key tools in Connect by MakeMyTrip (the partner dashboard): Smart Deals (fund a 5-8% discount to rise in search); value-add promotions (breakfast, late checkout); MMTBLACK Platinum member rates (closed-group, no parity breach). Your Content Score in Connect directly affects your ranking. See the guide to improving MMT ranking for the full checklist. MMT has dedicated categories for Homestays and Villas and Couple-Friendly Hotels: set your property type correctly to appear in these searches.

Commission: most independent Goa hotels pay 18 to 22%. On a ₹5,000 room night that is ₹900 to ₹1,100. Promotional participation adds to this.

Booking.com for Goa Hotels

Booking.com is your primary channel for UK and European guests, Israeli travellers, and Gulf visitors. The Arambol-Anjuna-Vagator backpacker belt uses Booking.com alongside Hostelworld. South Goa (Colva, Benaulim, Palolem) draws more international guests than North Goa, so for a South Goa beach resort, Booking.com often drives the larger share of high-value bookings.

Key tools: the Genius programme (10-20% member discount, funded by you, in exchange for a Genius badge and visibility boost; see the Genius programme guide); Visibility Booster (paid placement, additional commission); geo-targeted country rates (lower price shown only to guests browsing from a specific country). The Preferred Partner badge is awarded automatically to properties with strong scores and low cancellations.

If you have budget dorm beds or rooms under ₹2,000 per night in Arambol, Anjuna, or Vagator, also list on Hostelworld. Its audience is Israeli and European backpackers planning multi-week stays. Booking.com reaches a similar audience, so there is overlap, but Hostelworld users search there specifically for this property type.

Airbnb for Goa Villas and Homestays

Airbnb dominates the whole-property segment. Guests searching for a 3-bedroom villa in Assagao or a homestay in Palolem search on Airbnb first. Airbnb's category browse (Beachfront, Tropical, Unique Stays) surfaces your property to guests who never typed a specific location search.

Airbnb moved most hosts to a host-only fee (about 15.5% deducted from payout; verify your own dashboard). The Guest Favourite badge requires at least 5 reviews, an average above 4.9 stars, and strong cleanliness and accuracy scores; Airbnb checks eligibility daily. Registration note: Goa's Department of Tourism began enforcement in April 2026, with inspections and closures for properties listed on Airbnb without a valid Goa Registration of Tourist Trade certificate. Register at goatourism.gov.in before listing.

Agoda for Goa Hotels

Agoda's audience in Goa is Southeast Asian (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand), Australian, and Gulf travellers. It is the right channel if your property is near Manohar International Airport (Mopa, code GOX) in North Goa or you see meaningful Asian traffic in your booking data. Agoda is part of Booking Holdings, so inventory can overlap with Booking.com. Agoda Growth Express (AGX) lets you increase your commission to boost ranking without a new contract; check Agoda's partner hub for current eligibility. The STAAH 2025 national chart ranks Agoda second by room nights nationally, but in Goa, domestic volume is so large that MMT's Goa share is much higher in practice.

Direct Booking in Goa: WhatsApp, Instagram and Repeat Guests

Direct booking is a channel, not a fallback. In Goa it has advantages no OTA can match:

  • Repeat villa guests: A guest who stayed in December often wants the same property next year. A WhatsApp booking with a small loyalty discount converts a commission-paying OTA guest into a zero-cost return.
  • Long stays: Guests staying 7+ nights prefer to negotiate directly. You can offer a long-stay rate that still beats the OTA net after commission.
  • Russian guests: Direct WhatsApp and Instagram is the only viable channel for returning Russian guests. Keep every returning Russian guest's contact.
  • Google Free Booking Links: your direct rate appears alongside OTA rates in Google Search at zero cost when a guest searches by name. Read the Free Booking Links guide to connect your booking engine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Goa Tourism registration before listing on OTAs?
Yes. All accommodations operating commercially in Goa, including villas and homestays listed on Airbnb, must register under the Goa Registration of Tourist Trade Act. Enforcement began in April 2026, with inspections and closure notices for unregistered properties. Register at goatourism.gov.in before listing on any OTA.
Can I list my Goa villa on both Airbnb and MakeMyTrip at the same time?
Yes. Airbnb reaches international leisure guests; MakeMyTrip reaches domestic guests via the Homestays and Villas category. The two audiences barely overlap. Use a channel manager to sync availability in real time, otherwise a double booking is a matter of when, not if.
Which is better for a South Goa beach resort: Booking.com or MakeMyTrip?
Both, for different guests. MakeMyTrip drives domestic couples, groups, and families. Booking.com drives UK and European guests who stay longer and spend more per night. Run both channels and use channel-specific promotions to manage demand on each.
How do I reach Russian tourists when Booking.com and Expedia do not serve Russia?
Russian guests reach Goa via charter tour operators, not OTAs. Contact Goa-based inbound operators who hold Aeroflot charter contracts for the Morjim/Ashwem belt. Once a Russian guest has stayed with you, maintain direct WhatsApp contact for future years.
Is Hostelworld worth it for a small Goa guesthouse?
List on Hostelworld if you have dorm beds or budget rooms under roughly ₹2,000 per night in Arambol, Anjuna, Vagator, or Assagao. Its audience is Israeli and European backpackers planning multi-week stays. If you target couples or families, Booking.com and MMT will serve you better.
What is the commission difference between Airbnb and MakeMyTrip for a Goa villa?
Airbnb deducts about 15.5% from your payout. MakeMyTrip charges 15 to 25%, with most independent villas paying 18 to 22%. On a villa night at ₹8,000: MMT takes roughly ₹1,440 to ₹1,760 and Airbnb takes about ₹1,240. Both charge 18% GST on their commission additionally. Use the OTA Commission Calculator to compare your exact net payout.
Which OTA is best for Goa hotels during the off-season (June to September)?
MakeMyTrip and Goibibo drive domestic monsoon staycation bookings from Pune, Mumbai, and Bengaluru. Booking.com brings some European guests who prefer the quieter season. Activate Smart Deals on MMT, drop your minimum length of stay to 1 night, and use WhatsApp for personalised packages to repeat guests.
