MakeMyTrip Hotel Registration: How to List Your Hotel on MMT

By OnlineHotelier Insights Team Updated September 2026 9 min read

MakeMyTrip Hotel Registration in Short

Register free at partners.makemytrip.com, live in 3 to 7 days

Registering your hotel on MakeMyTrip (MMT) costs nothing. You sign up on MMT Partner Central, upload your GSTIN, PAN, cancelled cheque and trade licence, and wait 3 to 7 working days for KYC approval. You only pay once a booking comes in, as commission of 12 to 23 percent. On a ₹3,000 booking at 18 percent, that is ₹540.

New to OTAs? Start here. If this is your first listing, read what an OTA is and how it works first. It explains commission, extranets and rate parity before you commit to a contract.

What You Need Before You Register on MakeMyTrip

Most rejected applications fail on paperwork, not on the property. Keep these ready as scans before you start, and registration takes one sitting instead of three.

Requirement What MMT Expects
GSTIN certificate Required if your turnover crosses the GST threshold. Small homestays below it can register with a declaration instead.
PAN card Of the hotel entity, or of the proprietor for a sole proprietorship.
Cancelled cheque Bank account name must match the PAN name, or payouts get held.
Trade licence or registration Municipal trade licence, shop and establishment certificate, or state tourism registration.
Property photos Minimum 10, at least 1280×960px. 25 or more converts noticeably better.
Registration fee None. MMT charges no joining fee. You pay commission only on bookings that come through.

Who Can Register

MMT accepts hotels, resorts, guest houses, homestays, villas and service apartments. Your property needs a physical address, at least one bookable room, and the legal right to accept paying guests. There is no minimum room count.

How to Register and List Your Hotel on MakeMyTrip: 5 Steps

1 Register on MMT Partner Central

Go to partners.makemytrip.com and click "Register as Hotel". This is the MMT hotel partner registration page, and it is free. Fill in your property name, city, room count and contact details. You will get an OTP on your registered mobile number.

Use an email and mobile number you will keep. Every booking alert, rate change and payout notice goes there, and changing them later needs a support ticket.

2 Complete KYC Verification

Upload the following documents:

  • GSTIN certificate
  • PAN card (hotel entity or proprietor)
  • Cancelled cheque or bank statement
  • Hotel registration / trade licence
  • Property photos (minimum 10, at least 1280×960px)

KYC approval takes 3 to 7 working days. MMT may call your property to verify the address and the contact person, so brief whoever answers the phone.

3 Set Up Your Property Listing

Once approved, complete your property profile: room types, amenities, policies (check-in time, pet policy, cancellation terms), and upload all photos. Incomplete listings rank lower in MMT search results.

4 Load Rates and Availability

Set your base rates for each room type and open inventory for the dates you want to sell. Use the MMT extranet or connect a channel manager to sync rates automatically.

5 Go Live and Monitor

Once content is complete and rates are loaded, your listing goes live. Monitor your ranking, review score, and conversion rate from the MMT Partner Central dashboard.

Expect a slow first month. New listings carry no reviews, and MMT ranks unreviewed properties lower. Most hotels see volume pick up after the first 5 to 10 reviews.

How Long MakeMyTrip Registration Takes, and What It Costs

Two questions come up before every listing. Here are the honest answers.

  • Time. Registration itself takes about 30 minutes. KYC approval takes 3 to 7 working days. Content and photo setup takes another day or two. Budget about two weeks from sign-up to your first booking.
  • Cost to register. Nothing. There is no joining fee, no annual fee and no listing charge.
  • Cost to sell. Commission of 12 to 23 percent per booking. On a ₹3,000 room night at 18 percent, MMT keeps ₹540 and you receive ₹2,460 before TDS.
  • Optional costs. Promotions and Smart Deals fund a further 5 to 8 percent discount out of your own rate. These are opt-in, not compulsory at sign-up.

Why Registrations Get Rejected or Delayed

  • Bank account name does not match the PAN name
  • Blurred or cropped document scans
  • Trade licence expired, which is the most common single reason
  • Fewer than 10 photos, or photos below 1280×960px
  • Property address on the licence does not match the address entered

MMT Extranet: Managing Your Listing Day-to-Day

The MMT extranet (Partner Central) is where you manage everything after going live:

Function Where to Find It
Update rates and availability Inventory & Rates section
View and manage bookings Reservations tab
Update photos and content Property Details section
View reviews and respond Reviews & Ratings tab
Download settlement reports Finance > Invoices
Create promotions and offers Promotions section
Pro Tip: If you list on more than 2 OTAs, managing MMT manually becomes time-consuming. A channel manager syncs rates and inventory to MMT automatically, one update, all channels updated.

Photos and Content That Drive More Bookings

MMT's algorithm ranks listings partly on content quality. Hotels with better content get more impressions and higher conversion rates.

Photo Requirements

  • Minimum 10 photos. 25 or more significantly improves conversion
  • Resolution: 1280×960px minimum (1920×1080px recommended)
  • Must include: room interior, bathroom, property exterior, common areas, dining area
  • No watermarks, no text overlays, no heavy filters

Content Checklist

  • Property description: 150+ words, mention key landmarks and distance from railway/airport
  • Amenities: Mark every available amenity accurately, misleading info leads to bad reviews
  • Room types: Create separate listings for each room category (Standard, Deluxe, Suite)
  • Policies: Set clear check-in/check-out time, cancellation policy, and extra bed charges

MakeMyTrip Commission, Payouts and TDS Deductions

Item Details
Base commission 12 to 23 percent depending on property type and city tier. On a ₹3,000 booking at 18 percent, that is ₹540.
Smart Deals / promotions Additional 5 to 8 percent discount funded by you (optional)
TDS deduction 1 percent on gross booking value (u/s 194-O). On ₹3,000, that is ₹30.
GST on commission 18 percent GST on MMT's commission. On ₹540 commission, that is ₹97.
Settlement cycle Weekly, 7 to 14 days after checkout
Payment mode NEFT/RTGS to registered bank account
Tax Note: MMT deducts TDS (1%) and GST on commission before remitting. Always reconcile your MMT settlement report against your accounts. See our TDS & TCS guide for OTA payments for the full breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my hotel on MakeMyTrip?
Register at partners.makemytrip.com with your GSTIN, PAN, bank details, and property documents. After KYC verification (3 to 7 days), set up your listing with photos and rates, then go live.
What commission does MakeMyTrip charge hotels?
MMT charges 12 to 23 percent depending on property type, city, and programs opted into. Budget hotels in tier-2 and tier-3 cities typically pay 18 to 20 percent. On a ₹3,000 booking at 18 percent, MMT keeps ₹540. TDS and GST on commission are deducted before settlement.
How long does MakeMyTrip hotel listing approval take?
KYC approval takes 3 to 7 working days after all documents are submitted. Your listing goes live once content and rates are complete.
How does MakeMyTrip pay hotels?
MMT settles weekly, 7 to 14 days after guest checkout, via NEFT/RTGS to your registered bank account after deducting commission, TDS, and applicable charges.
Is MakeMyTrip hotel registration free?
Yes. MakeMyTrip charges no joining fee, no annual fee and no listing charge. You pay only commission of 12 to 23 percent on bookings that come through the platform.
How do I add my hotel to MakeMyTrip?
Register at partners.makemytrip.com, complete KYC with your GSTIN, PAN, cancelled cheque and trade licence, then add room types, photos, policies and rates. Your property goes live once MMT approves the listing.
Can a homestay or small guest house register on MakeMyTrip?
Yes. MMT accepts homestays, guest houses, villas and service apartments, and there is no minimum room count. You still need a trade licence or state tourism registration and a bank account matching your PAN.
Why was my MakeMyTrip registration rejected?
The usual reasons are an expired trade licence, a bank account name that does not match the PAN name, blurred document scans, or fewer than 10 photos. Fix the document and resubmit through Partner Central.
What documents are needed to list on MakeMyTrip?
GSTIN certificate, PAN card, cancelled cheque, hotel registration or trade licence, and a minimum of 10 property photos at 1280×960px or higher.
