How We Score OTA Channels

Each channel is scored 1 to 5, then weighted: Presence 45%, Promotional tools 35%, Presentation 20%. Presence leads because pilgrimage and government guests search by availability and price. Promo tools rank higher than Delhi's 30% because festival fill is the main revenue lever on this corridor. Yatra takes the fifth slot for its corporate arm, Yatra for Business, which added 163 corporate clients in Q4 FY2026 (Yatra earnings, 2026). See the national OTA guide for the full methodology.

Lucknow's Demand Profile: Pilgrimage, Government, Buddhist Circuit and Weddings

Ayodhya pilgrimage. The Ram Mandir consecration in January 2024 transformed this corridor. UP government estimates put annual visitors at approximately 14 to 16 crore in 2024 (Business Standard, June 2025). Ram Navami, Diwali-Deepotsav, Parikrama Mela and Shravan month (July to August) are the peak dates. Lucknow, roughly 130 km from Ayodhya, absorbs overflow. Ayodhya's domestic-only airport (AYJ, 1.1 million passengers FY2024-25) limits international arrivals; most pilgrims come by train.

UP government travel. As the state capital, Lucknow generates steady weekday demand from secretariat departments, state PSUs and Vidhan Sabha sessions. These guests book through direct rate letters, not leisure OTAs. This angle is distinctly Lucknow's, unlike Delhi's central government demand.

Buddhist circuit gateway. Gorakhpur is the confirmed air gateway: Kushinagar Airport has had no scheduled service since November 2023 (RTI, Aaj Tak, January 2026). Buddhist pilgrims fly into Gorakhpur (GOP) and travel to Kushinagar, Sarnath and Kapilvastu. Those continuing to Lumbini cross at Sunauli/Sonauli (70 to 95 km north, Wikipedia). See the guide to attracting international guests for source-market detail.

Weddings. October to February brings room-block demand. One contextual note; Jaipur owns this angle in the series.

MakeMyTrip and Goibibo for Lucknow Hotels

One MMT listing distributes across Goibibo and Travelguru automatically. For this corridor, MMT is the primary search tool. Commission runs 15 to 25%; on a ₹3,500 room that is ₹525 to ₹875 per booking. Activate MMTBLACK member rates with breakfast during festival peaks to hold RevPAR (revenue per available room). Keep your GST invoice option on in the extranet and complete your corporate profile in Connect by MakeMyTrip. Lucknow's government travel flows mainly through direct rate letters; MMT serves the overspill.

Festival calendar action: Open your calendars on MMT at least 60 days before Ram Navami, Diwali-Deepotsav, Parikrama Mela and Shravan month. Late opens lose the early-booking demand that fills at the highest rates.

Booking.com for Lucknow Hotels

Booking.com reaches guests MMT misses: European independent travellers exploring Bara Imambara, and international Buddhist pilgrims routing through Lucknow to Gorakhpur. These book further ahead at higher ADR (average daily rate). Lucknow's Booking.com frame is heritage transit and Buddhist circuit, contrasting with Jaipur where it is the primary lever for the Rajasthan circuit and wedding-season European guests. Three actions: achieve Genius tier eligibility (10 reviews, 7.5 rating); add a proximity paragraph about Nawabi-era landmarks in your extranet; use Visibility Booster during inter-peak gaps (February to March, September to October).

Agoda for Lucknow Hotels

Agoda's strength is Southeast and South Asian Buddhist pilgrims. These guests fly into Gorakhpur (GOP), not Kushinagar (no scheduled service since late 2023). Source markets include, directionally, Thailand, Sri Lanka, Japan, South Korea and Myanmar. Agoda reaches them more effectively than Expedia, which targets North American travellers. The winter peak is October to November (Kathina and Kartik Purnima). Set up your Gorakhpur listing on Agoda's YCS extranet before this window. UP's June 2025 Homestay and B&B Policy allows formal registration for 1 to 8 room properties in Gorakhpur and Ayodhya (Outlook Traveller, June 2025); Airbnb and Booking.com are the appropriate platforms for registered B&B properties.

Ixigo for Lucknow, Gonda and Gorakhpur Hotels

Ixigo runs a direct partner programme called HELLO. In June 2026, ixigo acquired Brevistay (54.66% stake, ₹65.69 crore, Skift). As of Q2 FY2026-27, HELLO directly contracts more than 12,000 properties across 1,100-plus towns (inc42.com, October 2026).

  • IRCTC discovery: Ixigo powers hotel bookings on IRCTC (medianews4u.com, 2026). A HELLO property appears in IRCTC hotel search automatically. Getting on ixigo is getting on IRCTC, which matters on a train-dominant corridor.
  • Tier-2/3 reach: A Gonda or Gorakhpur budget lodge with low MMT visibility still surfaces on ixigo for rail travellers. Check ixigo's hotel partner portal for commission terms.

Direct Booking for Lucknow, Ayodhya and Gorakhpur Hotels

  • Google Free Booking Links: Connect your booking engine at zero commission. A ₹3,200 direct rate against a ₹3,500 MMT rate closes price-sensitive pilgrims.
  • WhatsApp for repeat pilgrims: Build a WhatsApp list from your guestbook. A broadcast 45 days before each peak fills rooms at zero commission. Temple-town yatra organisers refer guests the same way.
  • Secretariat rate letters: Send a formal rate agreement to UP secretariat departments and state PSU administrative officers. Your GSTIN and a clean invoice format are what government accounts check first.

A channel manager (software that syncs your rates and availability across all OTAs at once) prevents parity errors during festival surges. Read the channel manager guide before going live on more than two platforms. Check the GST on hotel rooms guide for tariff rate guidance.

Which OTA to Use by Location: Lucknow, Ayodhya, Gonda and Gorakhpur

Lucknow city centre: Hazratganj, Gomti Nagar and Alambagh

MMT dominates. Secretariat and Vidhan Sabha guests make a consistent weekday base. Set up your corporate profile in Connect by MakeMyTrip with GST invoicing. Booking.com adds reach for international heritage tourists visiting Bara Imambara. For Alambagh properties near Lucknow Junction, ixigo HELLO adds rail-traveller discovery. This week: add Booking.com if you have Nawabi character.

Ayodhya: Ram Mandir pilgrimage hub

MMT overwhelmingly dominates. Direct booking is second for repeat devotees. Ixigo HELLO is third: IRCTC hotel search surfaces ixigo listings to train-booking pilgrims. Booking.com adds little at the budget end. This week: open Ram Navami and Diwali-Deepotsav dates on MMT 60 days ahead; apply to ixigo HELLO. Small Ayodhya properties (1 to 8 rooms) can register formally under UP's June 2025 Homestay and B&B Policy (local body NOC required); Airbnb and Booking.com become available once registered.

Gonda: Ayodhya overflow, approximately 50 km away

Gonda is roughly 50 km from Ayodhya (Rome2Rio). On peak festival dates, Ayodhya fills and Gonda absorbs overflow. Hotel stock is budget-dominant, channel manager penetration is low and walk-in bookings remain high. This week: apply to ixigo HELLO first; once listed you appear in both ixigo and IRCTC hotel search. Then open your festival calendar on MMT at ₹200 to ₹300 below Ayodhya comparables to capture pilgrims who could not find a room closer.

Gorakhpur: Buddhist circuit gateway and Nepal border transit

Gorakhpur's mix differs from the rest of the corridor. International Buddhist pilgrims arrive at GOP airport and travel to Kushinagar, Sarnath and Kapilvastu; Agoda and Booking.com are the right channels for this segment. For domestic guests, MMT and ixigo lead. Sunauli/Sonauli (70 to 95 km north) is the main crossing to Lumbini; many of these guests book their Gorakhpur night via OTA on the day of arrival. This week: open Kathina and Kartik Purnima dates in Agoda's YCS extranet with a two-night minimum stay; add ixigo HELLO for rail-arriving domestic pilgrims.

Varanasi circuit note. Varanasi and Sarnath are the next stop on the Buddhist circuit from Gorakhpur; a dedicated guide is planned for this series.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which OTA should I list on first if my hotel is in Ayodhya?
Start with MakeMyTrip. Ayodhya's demand is almost entirely domestic, and MMT is where Indian devotees search for accommodation around Ram Navami, Parikrama Mela and Shravan month. Once live on MMT, add ixigo's HELLO platform to reach rail-arriving pilgrims who also appear in IRCTC hotel search. Booking.com adds little at the budget end; hold it until you are established on the two domestic channels.
Do I need UP Tourism registration before listing my hotel on MakeMyTrip or Booking.com?
No confirmed rule requires UP Tourism classification before an OTA listing. UPSTDC runs a voluntary portal (Platinum, Diamond, Gold, Silver, Bronze, opened December 2023); no mandatory pre-listing enforcement was found. Before listing, confirm you have: GST registration, FSSAI licence if you serve food, a Fire NOC and a municipal trade licence. Classification is worth pursuing for credibility, not as a prerequisite.
How do Lucknow hotel owners get UP government and secretariat bookings without an OTA?
Send a rate agreement letter to UP secretariat departments and state PSUs, addressed to the administrative officer, with your GST-compliant tariff and food plan. Google Free Booking Links (zero commission) let walk-in secretariat staff book direct from a Google search.
Is Agoda worth listing on for a Gorakhpur hotel near the Buddhist circuit?
Yes. Gorakhpur is the confirmed air gateway: Kushinagar International Airport has had no scheduled flights since November 2023. International pilgrims from Thailand, Sri Lanka, Japan, South Korea and Myanmar fly into Gorakhpur (GOP) via IndiGo, SpiceJet or Akasa Air. Agoda reaches these Southeast Asian markets better than any OTA available to Indian hoteliers. List on Agoda and Booking.com together and set up a season rate for the Kathina and Kartik Purnima winter peak.
What is ixigo's HELLO platform and is it worth listing on for a small Gonda hotel?
HELLO is ixigo's direct hotel partner programme, which as of Q2 FY2026-27 directly contracts more than 12,000 properties across 1,100-plus towns (inc42.com, October 2026). A Gonda hotel on HELLO appears on ixigo search and, because ixigo powers IRCTC hotel search, shows up on the IRCTC platform automatically. On the Lucknow-Ayodhya-Gorakhpur rail corridor this is a real discovery channel. Check ixigo's hotel partner portal for current commission terms.
How should a Lucknow hotel manage its OTA rates during Ayodhya peak festival periods?
Open all channels at least 60 days before Ram Navami (March-April), Diwali-Parikrama Mela and Shravan month (July-August). Raise rates 20 to 35% on MMT and activate MMTBLACK member rates with breakfast to hold RevPAR. Set a two-night minimum stay for Ram Navami weekend. Use a channel manager to push rate changes across all platforms at once; manual updates during a pilgrimage rush cause parity errors and OTA suppression. For GST implications see the GST on hotel rooms guide.
