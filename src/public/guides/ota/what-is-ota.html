OTA Full Form: Online Travel Agency Meaning for Indian Hotels

By OnlineHotelier Insights Team Updated October 2026 8 min read

OTA Full Form in Short

OTA full form: Online Travel Agency.

OTAs such as MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com and Agoda list your rooms and charge a commission of 15 to 25 percent per completed booking.

Also written as: ओटीए (Hindi/Marathi) · ఓటీఏ (Telugu)

What Does OTA Stand For?

Break the letters down and the business model becomes clear:

O
Online
Operates on the internet, 24/7
T
Travel
Covers hotels, flights, holidays
A
Agency
Acts as broker, earns commission

The word "agency" is intentional. Just like a real-estate agent earns a fee for connecting a buyer and seller, an OTA earns a commission for connecting a traveller and a hotel. The difference is that an OTA does this automatically, at scale, for millions of travellers every day.

Think of an OTA as the Amazon for hotel rooms. A traveller in Mumbai opens MakeMyTrip, types "Udaipur", sets their dates, and sees 200 hotels ranked by price, reviews, and availability. They compare, read photos, and book in two clicks. Your hotel did nothing during that booking process. The OTA handled everything.

How OTAs Work in India

Here is the basic flow step by step:

  1. Hotel lists rooms on the OTA with rates, photos, and availability
  2. Traveller searches for hotels by destination, dates, and budget
  3. Traveller books a room through the OTA platform
  4. OTA confirms the booking and notifies the hotel
  5. Guest stays at the hotel
  6. OTA deducts commission from the payment and settles the balance

After the guest checks out, the OTA deducts its commission and pays the hotel the balance. The hotel gets the guest, the OTA gets its fee.

OTAs are not optional for most Indian hotels. They are the primary discovery channel. The question is not whether to use them. It is how to use them without giving away too much margin. That is where a channel manager becomes essential: it lets you manage your listing on all OTAs from one dashboard instead of logging into each separately.

List of Major OTAs in India

The Go-MMT Group (MakeMyTrip and Goibibo combined) holds approximately 50 percent of India's domestic online hotel booking market. Booking.com leads for international visitors. For most Indian hotels, the priority order is:

OTA Strength Best For
MakeMyTrip / Goibibo Largest Indian OTA group (same parent) Domestic leisure travellers, all hotel types
Booking.com Global leader, strong brand trust International guests, boutique hotels
Agoda Strong in Asia-Pacific region Southeast and East Asian travellers
Cleartrip Growing Indian platform (Flipkart-owned) Domestic leisure and corporate
Expedia Global corporate travel network International corporate bookings
Airbnb Originally for homestays, now includes hotels Boutique hotels, unique properties, villas
Yatra Indian corporate travel specialist Corporate bookings, MICE

OTA Commission Rates in India (2026)

Commission is the price you pay for visibility. Here are the current ranges for Indian hotel contracts:

OTA Typical Commission Payment Cycle Notes
MakeMyTrip / Goibibo 15–25% Weekly (7–14 days post-checkout) Most independent hotels pay 18–22%. Smart Deals add extra.
Booking.com 15–18% Monthly invoice or guest-pay Preferred Partner program adds 3–5% on top
Agoda 15–22% Monthly post-checkout Part of Booking Holdings; shared inventory option
Cleartrip 12–18% Weekly to bi-weekly Corporate rates negotiable
Airbnb 3% (host fee) 24 hrs after check-in Guests pay a separate service fee
Expedia 15–25% Guest pays OTA or at hotel Covers Expedia.com, Hotels.com and partner platforms

Ranges reflect 2026 standard contracts. Properties with higher volume or strong review scores may negotiate lower rates directly with their OTA account manager.

Remember that TDS (0.1%) and TCS (0.5%) are also deducted from OTA settlements. See our TDS & TCS guide for details.

OTA Booking vs Direct Booking: The ₹5,000 Example

Numbers make this real. Take a room priced at ₹5,000 per night:

Direct
₹4,900 net (2% gateway fee)
Via OTA
₹4,000 net (20% commission)

The difference per booking is ₹900. Scale that to 1,000 room nights a year and you are leaving ₹9 lakh on the table annually. For a hotel doing 3,000 room nights a year with 60 percent OTA bookings, the gap can exceed ₹16 lakh.

Factor OTA Booking Direct Booking
Commission 15–25% 0% (only payment gateway fees ~2%)
Guest Data Limited (OTA controls the relationship) Full guest profile and contact
Visibility High (millions of users) Depends on your marketing
Repeat Bookings Guest may book through OTA again Can build loyalty directly

This is why hotels with a working direct booking strategy are consistently more profitable than those that rely entirely on OTAs. The OTA is a customer-acquisition channel, not a business model.

When OTAs Help Your Hotel (And When They Hurt)

OTAs are a tool, not a strategy. Used correctly, they fill rooms. Used without a plan, they erode margins.

OTAs help when...

  • Your property is new with no brand recognition
  • You need to fill rooms in the off-season
  • You want to reach international travellers
  • You are entering a competitive market and need reviews fast

OTAs hurt when...

  • Peak season demand is high and rooms fill anyway
  • Returning guests keep rebooking through the OTA instead of direct
  • You are running a wedding or MICE event (always go direct)
  • OTA dependency exceeds 70% of total bookings

How to Work Effectively with OTAs

  1. Maintain rate parity: Keep the same rates across all OTAs and your website
  2. Optimise your listing: High-quality photos, detailed descriptions, updated amenities
  3. Respond to reviews: Both positive and negative reviews need professional responses
  4. Use a channel manager: Sync rates and availability across all OTAs automatically
  5. Monitor your ranking: OTAs reward hotels with good conversion rates and reviews
  6. Leverage promotions strategically: Participate in OTA flash sales and visibility boosters only when you have rooms to fill

OTA Settlement and Payments

Understanding how OTAs pay you is essential for cash flow:

  • MakeMyTrip/Goibibo: Weekly settlement, 7–14 days after checkout
  • Booking.com: Monthly invoice or guest pays at hotel directly
  • Agoda: Monthly settlement after checkout
  • Cleartrip: Weekly to bi-weekly settlement

Reducing OTA Dependency

While OTAs bring visibility, over-dependence is risky. Aim for a healthy channel mix. Hotels that successfully reduce OTA dependency usually start with a booking engine that captures direct bookings at zero commission. Read our hotel direct booking strategy guide for a step-by-step plan.

  • Build a booking engine: Let guests book directly on your website
  • Collect guest emails: Send offers directly for repeat stays
  • Google Free Booking Links: Show your direct rate on Google Search and Maps for free. See our complete FBL guide.
  • Social media presence: Instagram and Facebook drive awareness
  • Corporate tie-ups: Direct contracts with companies bypass OTAs entirely

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the full form of OTA?
OTA full form is Online Travel Agency. The letter O stands for Online, T for Travel, and A for Agency. MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Agoda, and Cleartrip are all OTAs. They charge Indian hotels a commission of 15 to 25 percent per completed booking.
What is OTA in the hotel industry?
In the hotel industry, OTA stands for Online Travel Agency. It is a website or app where travellers search and book hotel rooms. The OTA lists your rooms, handles the booking, and deducts a commission of 15 to 25 percent from each completed stay. Examples in India include MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com, Agoda, and Cleartrip.
What is the OTA commission percentage in India?
OTA commissions in India typically range from 15 to 25 percent of the room rate. MakeMyTrip and Goibibo charge 15 to 25 percent (most independent hotels pay 18 to 22 percent). Booking.com charges 15 to 18 percent. Agoda charges 15 to 22 percent. Cleartrip charges 12 to 18 percent. Airbnb charges a flat 3 percent host fee. Commission is charged only when a guest completes a stay, not on cancellations.
What is the difference between OTA and direct booking?
A direct booking is when a guest books with the hotel directly: by phone, the hotel's own website, or email, with no OTA involved. On a room priced at ₹5,000, a direct booking nets approximately ₹4,900 (after a 2 percent payment gateway fee). The same booking via an OTA charging 20 percent nets ₹4,000. That ₹900 difference per booking adds up to ₹9 lakh a year on 1,000 room nights.
Which OTA is best for hotels in India?
For Indian hotels, the priority order is: (1) MakeMyTrip plus Goibibo: largest domestic OTA group, essential for Indian leisure travellers. (2) Booking.com: largest global OTA, dominant for international guests. (3) Agoda: strong for Southeast and East Asian travellers. (4) Cleartrip: growing domestic platform worth adding once the first three are running. Most hotels list on all four to maximise visibility.
Can a hotel survive without OTAs in India?
Possible but difficult for most properties. Established hotels with strong brand recognition, a direct booking engine, and corporate accounts can reduce OTA dependence significantly. For new, budget, or mid-market hotels, OTAs are the fastest path to bookings and reviews. The goal is a healthy channel mix: 40 to 60 percent OTA and 40 to 60 percent direct, not zero OTA presence.
