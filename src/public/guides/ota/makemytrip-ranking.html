How to Rank Higher on MakeMyTrip Without Dropping Your Room Rate

By OnlineHotelier Team Updated October 2026 8 min read

MMT Ranking in Short

6 levers that improve ranking without a price cut

MMT does not publish its algorithm. Its partner tools and hoteliers' experience point the same way: listing quality, guest satisfaction, and reliability shape placement. You do not need to drop your rate. The 6 levers are:

  1. Content Score: complete your listing in Connect by MakeMyTrip
  2. Photos: 20-plus quality images, right order
  3. Reviews: recent, positive, replied to
  4. Promotions: value-adds, not price cuts
  5. Cancellations: keep hotel-initiated cancellations at zero
  6. Availability: stay open, use rates not closures to manage demand
New to listing on MMT? Read how to list your hotel on MakeMyTrip first. It covers registration, photo specs, and getting your first booking.

A 24-room guesthouse near Calangute, Goa kept appearing on page two behind cheaper hotels. The owner cut the rate by ₹300. Bookings improved briefly, then flattened. The real problem: no neighbourhood description, 4 photos instead of 20, and blank amenity fields. A competitor at the same rate was winning because their listing was complete and their reviews were recent.

How Does MakeMyTrip Decide Which Hotels Appear First?

MMT does not publish its ranking algorithm. Connect by MakeMyTrip (the partner dashboard, formerly InGo-MMT, available as app and web) surfaces your Content Score and parity alerts. MMT's own partner resources and consistent hotelier experience point to these signals:

MMT ranking signals: what the platform sees and what you control

What Is the MMT Content Score and How Do You Raise It?

The Content Score is a real feature in Connect by MakeMyTrip. MMT's partner FAQ has dedicated articles on it: "What is the Content Score?", "How can I improve the Content Score?", and "How does my content score affect my listing?" MMT ties the score to how your listing performs, so treat it as the first number to raise.

Property Description: Keywords and Landmarks

Problem: The description says "clean rooms, good service, near beach" and describes every hotel in Goa.

Fix: Write 150 to 300 words naming your neighbourhood, nearby landmarks guests search for (Calangute Beach, Baga Road, Candolim market), your standout feature, and the guest type you suit. A guest searching "hotel near Baga Road" can only be matched to you if those words are in your listing.

Amenities and Room Types

Problem: Only Wi-Fi and parking are ticked. You have a pool, AC, and a rooftop restaurant, but none appear. You also have Deluxe, Super Deluxe, and Suite rooms, with only one category live on MMT.

Fix: Go through every amenity category in Connect and tick everything you genuinely offer. Each unchecked amenity is a search filter you vanish from. Then create a separate room-type entry for every category and complete check-in, check-out, and cancellation policies. Guests filter by policy type; a blank policy hurts conversion.

Which Photos Win the Click?

Photos are the first thing a guest sees on your listing card. A weak set costs you the click before they read a word.

Problem: Four photos: one blurry bedroom, one dark reception, two harsh-light exterior shots.

Fix: Lead with your best exterior or lobby shot (that is the search-results thumbnail). Shoot in natural morning or late-afternoon light. Cover every room type, bathroom, dining area, pool or garden, and one nearby landmark. We recommend 20-plus photos; a fuller gallery makes for a more complete listing. Check MMT's photo guidelines in Connect for size and aspect ratio requirements. For exact upload specs, see the MakeMyTrip hotel listing guide.

How Do Reviews Affect Your MakeMyTrip Rank?

Review score and recency both shape placement and clicks. In our experience a 4.5 from 80 recent reviews beats a 4.8 from 12 reviews two years old, because guests trust fresh feedback.

Problem: Guests check out happy and never leave a review. You have not replied to your last 30 reviews either.

Fix: Ask every departing guest directly: "A review on MakeMyTrip really helps us." Follow up with a WhatsApp message that evening. Reply to every review in Connect: two-sentence thank-you for positives, a brief factual response for negatives. Do not offer discounts or freebies for reviews; OTAs treat paid reviews as fake.

How to Join MMT Promotions Without Cutting Your Base Rate

The key distinction: some promotions add value to the stay; others require a price cut from your Best Available Rate (BAR). Start with value-adds.

Value-add offers include complimentary breakfast, late checkout, or early check-in added to a rate plan. Your room rate stays the same; the guest perceives more value. See the hotel rate plans guide for how to price CP, MAP and AP plans profitably.

MMTBLACK member rates: MMTBLACK is MMT's loyalty programme; MMTBLACK Platinum is the top tier (replaced MMTBLACK Exclusive in April 2024). Member-only discounts appear only to logged-in members, not the public, and not on other OTAs. Closed-group rates like these are usually treated differently from public rates for parity, but check your OTA contracts before relying on that. Check Connect for the member-rate options available to your property.

Early-booking and long-stay offers lock in revenue in advance and lower your per-night cost of acquisition. Set them up as a separate rate plan with conditions (book 30 days ahead, or stay 3-plus nights) so your BAR stays untouched for everyone else.

Free levers first. Commission and promotion participation can lift placement, but they cost money. Exhaust Content Score, photos, reviews, and low cancellations first. For what MMT takes per booking, see the MakeMyTrip commission guide.

What Happens to Your Rank When You Cancel a Booking?

Hotel-initiated cancellations are the fastest way to hurt your MMT ranking. Every cancellation strands a guest, and hoteliers consistently report visibility dropping after them.

Problem 1: Overbooking from stale inventory. You updated availability on one OTA but forgot MMT. A guest books a room you have already sold.

Fix: Use a channel manager (software that syncs room availability across all OTAs in real time). One booking on Booking.com instantly closes the room on MMT, Agoda, and Yatra.

Problem 2: Cancelling to resell at a higher rate. Peak demand spikes after a booking comes in at your standard rate, and you cancel to sell direct at a premium.

Fix: Do not. It breaks the booking you accepted, earns angry reviews, and risks action under your MMT agreement. Use BAR management to price peak dates correctly in advance.

Target: zero hotel-initiated cancellations. A channel manager costs roughly ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 per month for a small property. That is cheaper than the lost bookings and bad reviews that cancellations cause.

How Conversion, Availability and Rate Parity Affect Visibility

Like every OTA, MMT benefits when listings convert views into bookings. Closed dates convert nothing. Keep rooms available and use rate increases to protect margin; do not close the channel. If Booking.com or Agoda shows a lower rate than MMT, guests notice, conversion drops, and Connect may show a parity alert. A channel manager with rate management keeps all channels consistent without manual logins.

Your 30-Day MakeMyTrip Ranking Plan

Four weeks, free actions only, no rate cuts required.

Week 1: Fix the Listing

  • Note your Content Score in Connect by MakeMyTrip.
  • Rewrite your description: landmarks, neighbourhood, standout feature, guest type.
  • Tick every amenity you genuinely offer; create a room type for every category.
  • Complete all policies: check-in, check-out, cancellation.

Week 2: Photos

  • Shoot in natural light. A local photographer costs ₹2,000 to ₹5,000.
  • Upload 20-plus photos: exterior, every room type, bathroom, dining, pool or garden, one nearby landmark.
  • Set your lead photo to your best exterior or lobby shot.

Week 3: Reviews

  • Reply to every unanswered review in Connect.
  • Brief front desk to ask every departing guest for a MakeMyTrip review.
  • Set up a follow-up WhatsApp template to send the evening after checkout.

Week 4: Promotions and Inventory

  • Find one value-add promotion in Connect (breakfast, late checkout) and activate it.
  • Activate a channel manager if not already running; resolve any parity alerts.
  • Open availability for the next 90 days. Adjust rates on peak dates; do not close them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rank higher on MakeMyTrip without lowering my price?
Yes. MMT's ranking rewards content completeness, photo quality, review scores, low cancellation rates, and response behaviour. A hotel with a complete listing, 20-plus quality photos, and recent positive reviews can beat a cheaper hotel with a thin listing. Price is one input, not the only one.
What is the MMT Content Score?
The Content Score is a metric in the Connect by MakeMyTrip partner dashboard that measures how complete and detailed your listing is. It covers your property description, amenities, room types, photos, and policies. MMT's partner support has a dedicated article on how the score affects your listing, so raise it first.
Does MakeMyTrip penalise hotels for cancelling bookings?
MMT does not publish a penalty, but hoteliers consistently see visibility drop after hotel-initiated cancellations, and each one risks a bad review. The fix is keeping your inventory accurate in real time. Use a channel manager to sync availability across all OTAs and avoid the stale-inventory problem that causes most hotel-side cancellations.
Do reviews and review replies affect MMT ranking?
Yes. Your average score and how recent your reviews are both shape placement and clicks. Replies show future guests you listen. Respond to every review, positive or negative. Thank guests who leave good reviews. Address complaints factually and briefly; long defensive replies look worse than a short, calm acknowledgement.
Does paying higher commission improve ranking?
Commission and promotional participation can influence placement, but they are not the only levers. The free levers, including Content Score, photos, reviews, and low cancellations, should come first because they cost you nothing. Paying more commission to paper over a thin listing is an expensive way to rank when the same result is available from a better profile.
How long until ranking improves after making changes?
Your Content Score in Connect updates soon after you edit the listing. Ranking movement is slower and is not published by MMT. In our experience, give a full listing overhaul about 30 days before judging the result, and track bookings, not just position.
Does closing inventory on peak dates hurt my ranking?
Closed dates earn nothing and leave guests to book competitors. An occasional closure matters little, but closing repeatedly around festivals or long weekends is a pattern hoteliers often link to weaker visibility. Keep inventory open and use rate management to protect your margin on peak dates instead.
How do I appear in MMTBLACK deals?
MMTBLACK is MakeMyTrip's loyalty programme. MMTBLACK Platinum is the top tier, replacing MMTBLACK Exclusive since April 2024. Member-only discounts are shown only to logged-in MMTBLACK members, not to the general public. This lets you reward members without lowering your public rate. Check your OTA contracts on how closed-group rates count for parity. Check your Connect by MakeMyTrip dashboard for the member-only rate plan options available to your property.
Does rate parity with Booking.com matter on MMT?
Rate parity matters in two ways. First, MMT monitors whether your rates on competing OTAs are lower than on MMT. If they are, guests see it, conversion drops, and Connect may flag a parity alert. Second, some promotion types require your MMT rate to be competitive within the platform. Use a channel manager to keep rates consistent across channels, and check your Connect dashboard for any parity alerts.
