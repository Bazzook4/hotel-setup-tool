The Home

A home away from home, entirely yours

Casa Idana is not a hotel floor with a number on the door. It is a whole house, handed over to one family at a time, in a quiet residential pocket of Hiran Magri.

Three bedrooms, three bathrooms and a private living room mean everyone gets their own corner. The kitchen is fully equipped, so you can brew your own chai at 6am without waiting for room service.

Upstairs, the rooftop terrace is where most guests end up. With its own bar counter and firepit, it is built for long dinners, birthdays and small celebrations under an open Udaipur sky.
