The Villa

Not a room. The whole house.

Casa Idana is not a hotel floor with a number on the door. It is a whole villa in a quiet residential pocket of Hiran Magri, yours alone for the length of your stay.

Three bedrooms, three bathrooms and a private living room mean everyone gets their own corner. The kitchen is fully equipped, so you can brew your own chai at 6am without waiting for room service.

Upstairs, the rooftop terrace is where most guests end up. With its own bar counter and firepit, it is built for long dinners and small celebrations under an open Udaipur sky.
