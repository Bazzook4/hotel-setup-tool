Common Questions

Questions Hoteliers Ask Us First

Which OTA should my hotel list on first in India?

For most Indian hotels, MakeMyTrip and Goibibo bring the highest domestic volume, while Booking.com and Agoda matter more for international and metro travellers. The right starting point depends on your location, property type, and guest mix. Compare the options here.

Do I need a channel manager for a small hotel?

If you are live on more than two OTAs, a channel manager usually pays for itself by preventing overbookings and saving you from updating rates in each extranet separately. Below two channels, manual updates are often still manageable. Read the full explanation.

How much commission do OTAs charge hotels in India?

Indian OTA commissions typically run between 15 and 25 percent of the booking value, before GST, TDS, and TCS adjustments. What actually reaches your account is lower than the headline rate. Calculate your exact payout in ₹.

Is it better to get direct bookings or OTA bookings?

You need both. OTAs give you visibility and fill rooms you would not otherwise sell. Direct bookings carry no commission and let you own the guest relationship. Most healthy hotels use OTAs for reach while building direct bookings alongside. See how to balance the two.
