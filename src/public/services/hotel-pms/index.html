What is the OnlineHotelier PMS?

A hotel PMS (Property Management System) is the main software running your front desk. It records every booking, checks guests in and out, generates bills, tracks rooms, and produces the daily reports your manager needs each morning. Without one, you are either doing this on paper or juggling multiple registers and spreadsheets.

The OnlineHotelier PMS does all of that for free. It is a cloud-based system built for small independent hotels in India: budget hotels, guesthouses, boutique properties, hill station resorts, and homestays that have grown past the point where a notebook works.

Most commercial PMS software in India charges ₹1,500 to ₹4,000 per month. OnlineHotelier PMS is free to use. The only paid option is the channel manager add-on, covered separately below.

What the PMS includes

The features below cover typical day-to-day operations at an independent hotel. The owner has not finalised the feature list: confirm before publishing.

Reservations and booking calendar

View all bookings on a room-by-room calendar. Add, modify, or cancel reservations. Avoid double-bookings across your rooms.

Room inventory and room status

Track which rooms are occupied, vacant, dirty, or under maintenance. Give housekeeping and front desk a single live view.

Guest check-in and check-out

Record guest details at check-in, assign rooms, capture ID as required. Check out guests and generate the final bill in one step.

Guest profiles

Store guest history so repeat visitors are recognised at check-in. Useful for building loyalty with your regular guests.

Folio and billing

Post room charges, extra services, and payments to a guest's folio. Review the full bill before check-out and settle in one click.

GST invoices

Generate compliant GST invoices automatically. The system applies the correct slab: 5% for room tariff up to ₹7,500 per night (no ITC), and 18% above ₹7,500 per night (with ITC), effective 22 September 2025.

Housekeeping status

Update room status from housekeeping: dirty, clean, inspected. Front desk sees the update in real time so they know when rooms are ready.

Daily reports

Occupancy, ADR (average daily rate), and revenue for the day. Your manager gets the flash report they need each morning without building it manually.

How the PMS fits into your hotel's tech stack

A hotel PMS sits at the centre of your operations. Everything else connects to it.

  • Bookings from OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Booking.com) flow into the PMS via the channel manager add-on, or you can enter them manually.
  • Direct bookings from your website or walk-ins are entered at the front desk.
  • Housekeeping updates room status in the PMS after each clean.
  • Billing happens in the PMS: the guest's folio is settled at check-out and the GST invoice is generated automatically.
  • Your daily reports come from the PMS: occupancy, ADR, and revenue for the day.
