What is the OnlineHotelier Booking Engine?

A hotel booking engine is a tool that lets guests check room availability and make a reservation directly with your hotel, without going through an OTA. Most engines process payment at the time of booking using a payment gateway. Gateways charge 1.5 to 2% per transaction and require a merchant account to be set up.

The OnlineHotelier booking engine works differently. It is built for hotels in India where UPI is the natural way to pay. Your guests request a booking. Your hotel shares its own UPI or bank details. The guest pays your hotel directly. You see the payment in your own UPI app or bank statement, then confirm the reservation in the software.

This means no gateway to connect, no gateway fees on every transaction, and no commission to a third party. The money goes from the guest's account to your hotel's account.

Reservations from the booking engine go into the same OnlineHotelier system your front desk uses. One calendar, one place to see all your bookings: walk-ins, OTA reservations entered manually, and direct bookings from the engine.

Available to hotels in India. The UPI payment workflow is designed specifically for the Indian market. Any type of property can use it: a 10-room guesthouse in Jaipur, a hill-station resort in Mussoorie, a homestay in Goa, a budget hotel in Chennai. Your guests pay the same way they pay any other UPI contact.

How the UPI Booking Workflow Works

Four steps, from booking request to confirmed reservation.

1

Guest requests a booking

The guest fills in the booking form: dates, room type, number of guests. The request comes into your OnlineHotelier system as a pending reservation.

2

Hotel shares UPI details with the guest

You share your hotel's own UPI ID or bank account details with the guest. Think of it the way you would share bank details for any other payment or transfer. OnlineHotelier supplies the software only. It does not process, hold or settle any money.

3

Guest pays the hotel directly

The guest pays on your hotel's own UPI. The money goes straight to your account. No intermediary, no gateway, no third party between the guest's payment and your bank.

4

Hotel checks the payment and confirms in the software

You check the payment in your own UPI app or bank statement. Once you see it, you confirm the reservation in the OnlineHotelier software. The booking is now confirmed and shows in your calendar. This confirmation step is done by your hotel, not by the software. There is no automatic detection of incoming payments.

No automatic confirmation. Because there is no payment gateway, the software cannot detect when a payment arrives. Your front desk checks the UPI app or bank statement and then confirms the booking manually. This takes a minute and is the trade-off for having no PG fees. If your property needs bookings to confirm the moment payment is made, see the not the right fit section below.

Who it is for, and who it is not for

This is honest positioning. The UPI workflow works well for most Indian hotels, but not all situations.

Good fit
  • Hotels in India where UPI is already how guests pay
  • Independent properties of any size: budget hotels, guesthouses, homestays, resorts
  • Hotels that currently handle direct bookings by phone or WhatsApp
  • Properties where the front desk can check UPI and confirm within a few minutes
  • New properties setting up a direct booking channel for the first time
  • Hotels paying 15 to 20% OTA commission and wanting to shift some bookings direct
Not the right fit
  • If you need bookings to confirm automatically the moment payment arrives
  • If you need to accept credit or debit card payments at the time of booking
  • If your guests are predominantly international and cannot pay on Indian UPI

If you are not sure whether this fits your property's workflow, message us on WhatsApp. We will tell you honestly whether it is the right call or whether a standard booking engine with a payment gateway makes more sense for you.
