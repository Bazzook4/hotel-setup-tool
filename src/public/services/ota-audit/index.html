Free OTA Listing Audit

Send us your property name and we will look at your actual listings, your Google profile, and your rates across channels. You get back a plain list of what is costing you bookings, and what to fix first. No charge, no obligation to buy anything.

Usually back within 2 working days

What we look at

We check the things that quietly cost bookings. Most of these are invisible from inside your own extranet, which is why they go unnoticed for months.

Your OTA listings

  • Photo count, order, and whether the first image is doing its job
  • Description quality, and whether it reads like a listing or like filler
  • Amenities left unticked that guests filter by
  • Rate plans set up, or only one plan carrying everything
  • Cancellation policy, and whether it matches what you actually do
  • Review response rate, which affects ranking on most platforms

Your Google Business Profile

  • Whether it is claimed, or sitting unclaimed with wrong details
  • Name, address and phone matching what your OTA listings say
  • Photos and category, which decide whether you show in local search
  • Booking links, and whether Google can send enquiries to you at all

Your website and direct path

  • Whether you have a website, and whether it loads on a phone
  • How a guest who knows your name would actually reach you directly
  • What happens to an enquiry once someone sends one

Your rates across channels

  • Rate parity, meaning whether the same room shows different prices on different platforms
  • Where you sit against comparable properties nearby
  • Whether a discount programme is quietly undercutting your other channels

How it works

  1. You send us your property name and city on WhatsApp. Links to your OTA listings help but are not essential, we can find them.
  2. We look at your listings ourselves. This is a person checking your actual pages, not an automated score.
  3. You get a written summary on WhatsApp: what is working, what is costing you bookings, and the order we would fix it in.

What happens next is up to you. Most of what we find is fixable by you or your team without paying anyone. If you would rather we did it, we will tell you what that costs. If you would rather not, the audit is still yours to act on.

Why we do this for free

Two reasons, and neither is complicated.

The first is that we would rather show you what we know than tell you about it. If the audit is useful you will remember where it came from, and if you later need your listings set up properly or a website built, you will already know whether we are worth talking to.

The second is that it saves both of us time. By the time we have looked at your listings we know whether you need help at all, and you know whether we understood your property. That is a better starting point than a sales call.

We do these by hand, so there is a limit to how many we can take on in a week. If we are backed up we will tell you rather than send you something rushed.
